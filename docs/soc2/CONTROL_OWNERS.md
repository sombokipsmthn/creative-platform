# Control Ownership for KIPSMTHN Creative Platform

## Introduction

This document defines the ownership of controls within the KIPSMTHN Creative Platform SOC 2 program. Each control is assigned to a specific owner who is responsible for its implementation, operation, monitoring, and reporting.

## Control Ownership Structure

Control ownership is organized by Trust Services Criteria (TSC) category and control area. Each control owner is responsible for:

1. Ensuring controls are implemented according to specifications
2. Monitoring control effectiveness
3. Collecting and maintaining evidence
4. Reporting on control performance
5. Coordinating remediation efforts when needed
6. Ensuring controls are reviewed and updated regularly

## Control Owner Responsibilities

Each control owner has the following responsibilities:

1. **Implementation**: Ensure the control is implemented according to specifications
2. **Operation**: Ensure the control operates effectively on an ongoing basis
3. **Monitoring**: Monitor the control for effectiveness and compliance
4. **Evidence Collection**: Collect and maintain evidence of control operation
5. **Reporting**: Report on control performance to management and auditors
6. **Remediation**: Coordinate remediation efforts when control deficiencies are identified
7. **Review**: Regularly review and update the control as needed
8. **Training**: Ensure personnel receive appropriate training on the control
9. **Documentation**: Maintain documentation of the control and its operation
10. **Coordination**: Coordinate with other control owners as needed

## Evidence Requirements

Each control owner must maintain the following evidence:

1. Policies and procedures related to the control
2. Configuration settings and parameters
3. Monitoring logs and reports
4. Test results and validation records
5. Incident reports and remediation records
6. Training records and materials
7. Audit reports and findings
8. Change management records
9. Vendor assessments and reports
10. Risk assessment and treatment records

## Acceptance Criteria

The following criteria must be met for control ownership to be considered complete:

1. Every control has an assigned owner.
2. Each owner understands their responsibilities for the control.
3. Evidence collection procedures are defined for each control.
4. Reporting mechanisms are established for each control.
5. Remediation processes are defined for each control.
6. Review schedules are established for each control.
7. Training requirements are identified for each control.
8. Documentation standards are established for each control.

This control ownership document assigns responsibility for each control within the SOC 2 program to specific individuals or roles. It defines the responsibilities of control owners, evidence requirements, and acceptance criteria. This document serves as a foundation for accountability, monitoring, and reporting within the SOC 2 program.
