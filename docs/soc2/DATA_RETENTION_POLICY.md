# Data Retention & Disposal Policy

**Document Version**: 1.0
**Last Updated**: 2026-10-01
**Owner**: Engineering Manager
**Review Cycle**: Annually or when legal requirements change

---

## Purpose

This policy establishes retention and disposal requirements for data processed by the KIPSMTHN Creative Platform. It ensures data is retained only as long as necessary for business and legal purposes, and disposed of securely when no longer needed.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all data:

- Stored in the KIPSMTHN Creative Platform database (Neon)
- Stored in Vercel Blob or Cloudflare R2 storage
- Generated by application logs and analytics
- Held by employees or vendors on behalf of KIPSMTHN

## Retention Principles

### Minimum Necessary
- Retain data only for the minimum period necessary
- Regular review of retention requirements
- Automatic deletion when retention period expires

### Legal Compliance
- Meet all statutory retention requirements
- Comply with tax, accounting, and employment law
- Document legal basis for each retention period

### Business Necessity
- Retain data necessary for business operations
- Document business justification for retention
- Balance retention needs against privacy rights

### Data Minimization
- Do not retain data beyond necessity
- Anonymize or aggregate data when possible
- Delete or mask personal data when no longer needed

## Disposal Methods

### Secure Deletion Standards

| Data Type | Disposal Method | Verification |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| **Electronic data (databases)** | Multiple overwrite passes (3x minimum) | checksum verification |
| **Electronic data (storage)** | Cryptographic erasure or secure wipe | Deletion confirmation |
| **Backup media** | Secure deletion after backup retention expires | Log verification |
| **Log files** | Automated deletion after retention period | Log review |
| **Physical media** | Shredding or degaussing | Certificate of destruction |

### Deletion Procedures

**Automated Deletion**:
1. Scheduled jobs delete expired records
2. Verification logs created for each deletion batch
3. Monthly audit of deletion effectiveness

**Manual Deletion**:
1. Authorized personnel perform deletion
2. Dual verification for Restricted data
3. Documentation of deletion rationale

**Cryptographic Erasure**:
1. Destroy encryption keys
2. Verify data is inaccessible
3. Document key destruction

## Data Lifecycle Management

### Creation
- Classify data at point of creation
- Assign retention period based on classification
- Document data owner and purpose

### Active Use
- Monitor data usage patterns
- Review retention necessity periodically
- Update classification as needed

### Archival
- Move to archival storage when no longer actively used
- Apply archival security controls
- Document archival location and access

### Disposal
- Execute deletion per retention schedule
- Verify deletion completion
- Document disposal actions
- Update data inventory

## Special Considerations

### Legal Hold
When data is subject to legal hold:

1. **Identification**: Determine if data is involved in litigation or investigation
2. **Notification**: Inform data owners and IT staff
3. **Preservation**: Suspend automatic deletion
4. **Documentation**: Record legal hold rationale and duration
5. **Release**: Resume deletion when hold is lifted

### Data Subject Requests
When responding to data subject requests:

1. **Access Requests**: Locate and provide all data about the individual
2. **Correction Requests**: Update inaccurate data
3. **Deletion Requests**: Delete data unless legal retention applies
4. **Portability Requests**: Export data in portable format

### Cross-Jurisdictional Requirements
When data is subject to multiple jurisdictions:

1. **Assess** retention requirements for each jurisdiction
2. **Apply** the most restrictive requirement
3. **Document** jurisdictional analysis
4. **Review** regularly as laws change

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Data Owners
- Define retention periods for data they own
- Approve exceptions to retention policy
- Ensure timely disposal of data
- Maintain data inventory

### IT/Engineering
- Implement automated retention and deletion
- Monitor deletion effectiveness
- Maintain backup deletion schedules
- Generate deletion reports

### Legal/Compliance
- Define legal retention requirements
- Review and update retention schedule
- Advise on legal holds
- Manage data subject requests

### Security
- Verify secure disposal methods
- Audit deletion processes
- Monitor for data retention violations
- Report compliance gaps

---

## Monitoring and Reporting

### Key Metrics
- **Deletion Compliance Rate**: % of data deleted on schedule
- **Over-Retention Incidents**: Number of data retained beyond schedule
- **Disposal Verification Success**: % of deletions verified successful
- **Legal Hold Coverage**: % of potentially relevant data under hold

### Reporting
- Monthly deletion compliance report
- Quarterly retention review
- Annual policy effectiveness assessment
- Ad-hoc reports for audits
